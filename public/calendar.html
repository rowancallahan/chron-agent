Read my Google Calendar

The agent can look at your calendar (it can't change it) when you ask things like "what's on tomorrow?" It takes a minute, once, on a computer.

  1. Open Google Calendar on a computer and click the gear, then Settings.
  2. On the left, under Settings for my calendars, click your calendar, then Integrate calendar.
  3. Copy the Secret address in iCal format (it starts with https://calendar.google.com/calendar/ical/).
  4. Paste it below and tap Save.

Keep that link private: anyone who has it can see your calendar. It's kept only in your account here, and the agent never sees it, only your events.
