Notifications on iPhone

iPhones only send notifications from apps on the Home Screen, so add this site there first. It takes a minute, once.

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open the app from your Home Screen (the clock icon) and sign in.
  4. Go to Settings and tick Push on this device. Tap Allow when asked.
  5. Tap Test. A notification should pop up.

Nothing showing up? Check the iPhone's Settings → Notifications → chron, and make sure Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't on.
